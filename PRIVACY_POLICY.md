# Privacy Policy for NDEB AFK Prep Pro

**Last Updated:** October 8, 2026

Welcome to **NDEB AFK Prep Pro**. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

## 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
This application operates primarily offline using on-device local storage. We do not require you to create an account, register an email address, or provide personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, phone number, or physical address.

## 2. On-Device Study Data
Your study progress, quiz scores, spaced repetition schedules, flagged questions, and personal bookmarks are saved strictly on your local device (using your device's browser `LocalStorage`). This data is never transmitted to or stored on external database servers.

## 3. Subscriptions and Payments
In-app subscription transactions are processed directly and securely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play Store) and managed via RevenueCat. 
- We do not have access to, collect, or store your credit card or billing details.
- RevenueCat uses an anonymous, randomly generated App User ID solely to verify your active subscription entitlements across app sessions.

## 4. Third-Party Services
We do not sell, rent, or monetize your data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

## 5. Data Deletion
You maintain full control over your data. You can completely erase your local progress, quiz history, and statistics at any time by selecting "Reset All Progress" within the app dashboard or clearing your device's app storage.

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official App Store / Google Play support channels.
