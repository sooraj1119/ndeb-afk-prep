Privacy Policy

Dental Prep Pro: for NDEB AFK • Last Updated: October 2026 • Published by Dental Prep Pro Publishing

Welcome to Dental Prep Pro: for NDEB AFK. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it in compliance with the Canadian Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Zero Direct Personal Data Collection (No PII)

This Application operates with an offline-first architecture. We do not require you to create an account, register an email address, or provide personal identifiable information (PII) such as your legal name, physical address, or phone number.

2. On-Device Study Progress & Metrics

Your study progress, quiz scores, SM-2 spaced repetition schedules, flagged questions, and custom bookmarks are saved strictly on your local device (using device local storage). This data is never transmitted to, stored on, or monetized by external servers.

3. Subscriptions & Cross-Border Processing

In-app subscription transactions are processed directly and securely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play Store) and managed via RevenueCat Inc. We never have access to, collect, or store your credit card or billing details. RevenueCat uses an anonymous, randomly generated App User ID solely to verify active subscriptions. Cross-Border Notice: Subscription validation tokens are processed on secure servers located in the United States.

4. Legal Bases for Processing (EU GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), processing is based on Contractual Necessity (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) to unlock paid Pro study modules, and Legitimate Interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) to ensure application integrity.

5. Statutory Privacy Rights & Data Deletion

In accordance with PIPEDA, GDPR, and CCPA, you maintain complete control over your data. You can completely erase your local progress, quiz history, and statistics at any time by selecting “Reset All Progress” within the app settings. We do not sell or share personal data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

6. Child Privacy (COPPA & Age Restriction)

The Application is strictly intended for adult dental graduates and healthcare candidates preparing for professional licensure. It is not directed to individuals under 18 years of age (or under 16 in the European Union).

7. Designated Privacy Officer & Contact

In accordance with Canadian PIPEDA Principle 4.1.4, our designated Privacy Officer is accountable for data protection governance. For questions regarding this policy, contact: ndebpreppro@gmail.com.
