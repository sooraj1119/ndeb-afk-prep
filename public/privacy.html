Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining the highest standards of data protection. This Privacy Policy outlines how NDEB AFK Prep Pro handles information and complies with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

1. Zero Personal Data Collection (No PII)

This application does not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your legal name, physical address, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise geolocation.

2. On-Device Local Data Storage

All your educational progress data—including completed questions, quiz scores, flagged questions, spaced repetition intervals, and streak records—is stored exclusively on your device using local sandboxed storage (localStorage). Your study records never leave your device and are never transmitted to any central database or third-party advertising network.

3. Right to Erasure / Complete Data Deletion

In accordance with PIPEDA and GDPR rights, you maintain complete ownership and control of your data. You may permanently delete and wipe all stored progress, statistics, and history at any time by tapping "Reset All Progress" in the Dashboard or by uninstalling the application from your device.

4. In-App Purchases & Payment Processing

All financial transactions, subscriptions, and purchases are processed directly and securely through Google Play Billing (Google Play Store) or Apple In-App Purchases (Apple App Store). We do not collect, access, or store credit card numbers, billing addresses, or banking details.

We utilize RevenueCat as an anonymous subscription verification layer. RevenueCat generates an anonymized, pseudonymous App User ID solely to verify whether an active subscription entitlement exists with Google Play or Apple.

5. Third-Party Services & Permissions

The application requests only basic internet access (android.permission.INTERNET) to load question assets and communicate with official app store billing APIs. The application requests zero sensitive permissions (no location, no camera, no contacts, no microphone, and no storage permissions).

6. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions or inquiries regarding your privacy or data protection practices, please contact our support team at:
