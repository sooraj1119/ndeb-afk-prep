## Strict NDEB AFK Question Generation Protocol
When generating or reviewing multiple-choice questions for the NDEB AFK Prep app, you MUST adhere to the following strict guidelines:
1. **Format**: Use the standard NDEB clinical scenario structure (patient age, gender, symptoms, clinical/radiographic findings) OR theoretical fundamental knowledge questions.
2. **Single Best Answer**: Exactly 4 options. Only ONE is definitively correct. 
3. **Plausible Distractors**: Incorrect options must be plausible misconceptions, alternative diagnoses, or related but incorrect treatments. No "joke" or obviously wrong options.
4. **NO "All of the above" or "None of the above"** (NDEB explicitly avoids these).
5. **Explanations**: The explanation MUST be 2-3 sentences detailing exactly why the correct answer is right AND why the major distractors are wrong according to Canadian dental standards.
6. **NO DUPLICATES**: Must generate entirely unique clinical scenarios and question stems (checked via strict algorithmic deduplication).
</RULE[ndeb_strict_guidelines]>
